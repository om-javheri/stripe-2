Case studies

Brightwheel grows from startup to vertical SaaS category leader

Brightwheel is an early education technology company that provides childcare management software and embedded financial tools for childcare centres, preschools, educators and families across the US and Canada.

Challenge

Brightwheel needed to simplify provider onboarding, handle identity verification and compliance at scale, enable reliable tuition collection online and improve cash flow for the educators on its platform – all without building bespoke payments infrastructure for every new market.

Solution

Brightwheel built its payments and financial infrastructure on Stripe, using embedded onboarding components and automated compliance to cut provider verification to roughly one day. It enabled multiple payment methods and next-day payouts and expanded into Canada without rebuilding its payments stack – scaling to serve millions of students and families across two countries.

How INDY powers the future of independent movie theatres

INDY Cinema Group is a Scotland-founded cinema operating platform for independent movie theatres, including ticketing, concessions, memberships, marketing and payments.

Challenge

INDY needed to help local theatres compete with multiplex chains and streaming services. Theatre operators were using multiple fragmented systems for ticketing, concessions and memberships, creating operational inefficiencies and an inconsistent customer experience across every touchpoint.

Solution

INDY embedded Stripe throughout its platform to unify online and in-person commerce – launching with Stripe Payments and Connect, then adding Terminal with handheld readers for in-person ordering and Apps on Devices for dine-in workflows. This gave cinemas a single view of transaction data, simplified refunds and subscriptions and enabled expansion into new markets.

Venue.ink grows to 10,000 tattoo artists with Stripe

Canada-based Venue offers tattoo artists and studios an all-in-one platform to run their business, including marketing, scheduling, communications and payments.

Challenge

Tattoo artists were running modern businesses with fragmented legacy tools – managing bookings on Instagram, collecting deposits via peer-to-peer apps and handling final payments in person. Venue needed a payments partner it could launch with quickly and rely on as its all-in-one platform for scheduling, marketing and payments continued to grow.

Solution

Venue integrated Connect to manage funds flow and streamline KYC-compliant artist onboarding in minutes. It implemented Stripe's payment element to accept cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and BNPL options and it added Terminal for in-person payments and Instant Payouts – giving artists fast access to their earnings and a seamless end-to-end experience.

SiteMinder expands to serve hotels in 150 countries with the help of Stripe

SiteMinder is an award-winning hotel technology company based in Sydney, Australia, that brings enterprise hotel tech solutions to more than 50,000 customers.

Challenge

SiteMinder needed payments infrastructure that could handle complex multichannel booking flows, support rapid international expansion without building country-by-country compliance and licensing from scratch and reduce fraud – all while offering the local payment methods that regional hotel markets expect.

Solution

SiteMinder integrated Connect to route funds and simplify global merchant onboarding and it added Payments to support local payment methods like Apple Pay, Google Pay and iDEAL. It layered in Radar for AI-powered fraud detection and, most recently, Terminal to unify online and in-person payments – enabling a 90% onboarding success rate and a 61% reduction in fraudulent payment volume.
