Alma works with Enterprise support to reduce costs

Alma is a financing and payment provider that allows customers to pay for their purchases in instalments.

Challenge

Alma has grown quickly since launching in 2018, and now has over 2.4 million customers in 10 countries in Europe, including France, Germany, Belgium, Spain, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Portugal, Austria and Ireland. Alma looked to Enterprise support to reduce costs and continuously improve its Stripe integration.

Solution

With expert guidance from Stripe, Alma saved €300,000 in annual processing costs and reduced scheme fees by 57%. Through proactive monitoring and refined retry strategies, Alma also halved its API errors – without compromising payment success rates.

FICO partners with Stripe experts to improve their billing system

FICO created modern credit risk scoring for the financial services industry in the 1960s, and now offers both consumer and B2B services.

Challenge

FICO offers B2B analytics software and myFICO – a consumer-facing service that helps people understand their credit. FICO turned to its Stripe support plan for help improving subscription management, optimising its implementation and making quick adjustments to pricing and payment methods.

Solution

By partnering with Stripe experts, FICO halved the time spent managing billing questions, allowing its team to focus on its core product. This partnership also delivered a one percentage point lift in authorisation rates, maximising revenue capture across its global platform.

LG Electronics Japan uses its support plan to fight fraud

LG Electronics Japan is the Japanese subsidiary of LG Electronics, one of the world's leading consumer electronics companies.

Challenge

When LG Electronics Japan launched its online store, unexpected fraud on high-value products quickly diverted resources from growth. With the holiday sales season approaching, the team needed expert help to solve fraud without sacrificing revenue.

Solution

LG's Stripe Support team configured Radar with precise 3D Secure rules. As Fraud tactics evolved, the team continuously analysed data and guided LG through adjustments, helping the Company stay ahead of emerging threats. The result: LG completed its holiday sales with zero Fraud-related chargebacks.
